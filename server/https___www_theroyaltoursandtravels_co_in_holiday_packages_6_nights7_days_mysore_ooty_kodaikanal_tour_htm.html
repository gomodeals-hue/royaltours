Mysore, Kodaikanal and Ooty Tour Itinerary

Day 1

Bangalore To Mysore
Welcome to your 'South India Delight' tour. On arrival at the airport / railway station in Bengaluru, you will be met and transferred to your hotel in Mysore, en route visit spectacular fortress of Srirangapatna, Other attractions include Tipu's beautifully painted summer palace and Gumbaz, the family mausoleum.
In the evening, visit the Brindavan Gardens, The main attraction of Brindavan Gardens is its illuminated musical fountain, the water and lights of which are synchronised to the rhythmic beats of Indian music. Overnight stay at Mysore.
Day 2

Mysore
Today, after breakfast visit the famous Mysore Palace, which is a historical monument located in the city of Mysore facing the Chamundi Hills eastwards.

The rest of the evening is at leisure for various independent activities. Overnight stay at Mysore.
Day 3

Mysore To Ooty
Today, your picturesque journey continues into the blue Nilgiri Hills, as you drive through the steep hills covered with lush green tea and coffee plantations. En route visit the Chamundeshwari Temple, situated on the hill in the outskirts of Mysore.

On arrival, check in to the hotel at Ooty. Visit Ooty lake and Botanical Garden. Overnight stay in Ooty
Day 4

Ooty
This morning after breakfast, proceed for a sightseeing tour of Ooty, which includes a visit to Tea Museum & Factory. Later, drive to Coonoor and visit Sims Park that houses naturally occurring flora as well as trees from around the world. Also, visit the Dolphin's Nose Point, an enormous rock formation that resembles a dolphin's nose and Doddabetta peak. Overnight stay in Ooty
Day 5

Ooty To Kodaikanal
Today, your picturesque journey continues through the Palani Hills to another scenic hill-station- Kodaikanal. On arrival, check in to the hotel at Kodaikanal.

The rest of the day is at leisure to explore the quaint hill. Overnight stay in Kodaikanal.
Day 6

Kodaikanal
This morning after breakfast, proceed for a sightseeing tour of Kodaikanal, which includes a visit to Pillar Rocks, Coaker's Walk and the magnificent Bear Shola Falls.

In the afternoon, spend some time in Bryant Park - one of the most colourful attractions of Kodaikanal. Overnight stay in Kodaikanal.
Day 7

DEPART KODAIKANAL – BANGALORE
Today, bid farewell to your 'South India Delight' tour, as you are transferred from hotel in Kodaikanal to the airport / railway station in Bangalore/Coimbatore for your onward journey.
