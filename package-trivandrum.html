Trip Overview

Journey to India's southernmost land's end with The Royal Tours & Travels. Visit the magnificent Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, relax at Kovalam’s crescent lighthouse beach, explore the historic Padmanabhapuram Teakwood Palace, take the ferry to Vivekananda Rock Memorial & Thiruvalluvar Statue, and watch the breathtaking sunset over the confluence of three oceans.

Duration 4 Days / 3 Nights
Stay Beachfront & City Hotels
Vehicle Private AC Sedan
Meals Daily Breakfast

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Day 1: Arrival in Trivandrum & Kovalam Beach Sunset

Pick up from Trivandrum Airport / Railway Station. Transfer to beach resort in Kovalam. Spend the evening relaxing at Lighthouse Beach and Hawa Beach.

2

Day 2: Padmanabhaswamy Temple & Napier Museum

Early morning darshan at the opulent Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple. Visit Kuthiramalika Palace Museum, Napier Museum, and Poovar Island backwater boat cruise through mangrove forests.

3

Day 3: Padmanabhapuram Palace & Kanyakumari Sunset

Drive to Kanyakumari via the historic Padmanabhapuram wooden palace. Ferry ride to Vivekananda Rock Memorial & 133-ft Thiruvalluvar Statue. Witness sunset over the Triveni Sangam (Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean).

4

Day 4: Sunrise over Ocean & Departure

Watch the magnificent sunrise over the ocean. Visit Bhagavathy Amman Temple and Gandhi Memorial. Transfer to Trivandrum / Kanyakumari Railway Station for departure.
