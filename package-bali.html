Trip Overview

Immerse yourself in the Island of the Gods with The Royal Tours & Travels. Experience lush Ubud jungle pool villas, Tegalalang rice terrace swings, cliffside sunset fire dance at Uluwatu Temple, speedboat day trip to Nusa Penida (Kelingking T-Rex Beach), and luxury water sports in Tanjung Benoa.

Duration 6 Days / 5 Nights
Stay Private Pool Villa + 4-Star Resort
Speedboat Nusa Penida Transfers
Visa Indonesia VoA Support

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Day 1: Arrival in Denpasar & Transfer to Ubud Jungle Villa

Pick up from Ngurah Rai Denpasar Airport with flower garland welcome. Transfer to your luxury private pool villa in Ubud. Enjoy a candlelit floating breakfast setup and spa session.

2

Day 2: Ubud Rice Terraces, Bali Swing & Kintamani Volcano

Ride the iconic jungle swing over Tegalalang Rice Terraces. Visit Luwak Coffee plantation, sacred Tirta Empul water spring, and enjoy lunch with panoramic Mount Batur volcano views.

3

Day 3: Nusa Penida Island Speedboat Day Tour

Board a morning high-speed catamaran to Nusa Penida Island. Explore the world-famous Kelingking T-Rex cliff beach, Broken Beach natural arch, and Angel's Billabong infinity pool.

4

Day 4: Tanjung Benoa Water Sports & Uluwatu Sunset Fire Dance

Parasailing, banana boat, and jet ski at Tanjung Benoa beach. Late afternoon visit to the 70-meter sea-cliff Uluwatu Temple followed by the hypnotic Kecak & Fire Dance performance.

5

Day 5: Tanah Lot Ocean Temple & Seminyak Beach Clubs

Visit the mystical offshore sea temple of Tanah Lot. Spend the afternoon shopping at Seminyak boutiques and enjoy sunset cocktails at Potato Head Beach Club.

6

Day 6: Bali Departure

Leisure morning breakfast, souvenir shopping at Krisna Oleh-Oleh, and private transfer to airport for departure flight.
